What happens to your archive

Starred articles are kept indefinitely. Unread articles are never purged: not after a year, not ever. Articles you have read and not starred are removed after 90 days, and only once every subscriber to that feed has read them too.

Delete your account and everything of yours goes with it: subscriptions, read state, saved articles, tokens. There is a test that fills every one of those tables and then asserts they are empty.

This is on the front page rather than buried in the privacy policy because it is the part that should decide whether you hand over five years of reading.

Questions people ask first

Is it free?

Yes. An account is free and follows up to 300 feeds. There is no card to enter and no trial that runs out.

Can I bring my feeds from Feedly or Inoreader?

Yes. Every RSS reader can export your subscriptions as one OPML file. Import it here and your feeds and folders arrive in one step. Step by step: from Feedly, from Inoreader.

Does it work on my phone?

It runs in any browser, on any device, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. Articles you have opened stay readable offline.

Does it use AI or decide what I see?

No. There are no summaries, no ranking and no recommendations. You see every article, newest first.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. Alo Reader is open source under AGPL-3.0, and the same stack runs on your own server from one compose file.
