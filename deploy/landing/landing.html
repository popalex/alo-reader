What happens to your archive

Starred articles are kept indefinitely. Unread articles are never purged: not after a year, not ever. Articles you have read and not starred are removed after 90 days, and only once every subscriber to that feed has read them too.

Delete your account and everything of yours goes with it: subscriptions, read state, saved articles, tokens. There is a test that fills every one of those tables and then asserts they are empty.

This is on the front page rather than buried in the privacy policy because it is the part that should decide whether you hand over five years of reading.
